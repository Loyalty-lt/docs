## Current month

Press <Ui>Current Month</Ui>. You see how much is used and what it will cost.

<Shot id="atsiskaitymai-menuo" />

- **Monthly plan** – what is included and how much is used. “Within the limit” – you pay nothing extra.
- **Usage** – each service on its own.
- **Pricing** – what will be on the invoice. The <Ui>Monthly Total</Ui> row is the estimate.

The arrows choose earlier months. The numbers refresh every hour.

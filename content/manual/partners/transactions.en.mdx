Every purchase and refund is here: from the till, a website or the API.

Open <Path>Transactions</Path>.

<Shot id="transakcijos-sarasas" />

A refund is a separate row with a minus. It reduces the turnover; the original purchase stays.

## Purchase page

In the list press the sheet icon on the right.

<Shot id="transakcijos-viena" />

- **Purchase breakdown** – what was paid for, which discounts, how many points for each part.
- **Customer** – their tier, current points, which purchase this is and how much they spent in total.
- **Staff member** and **Till** – who sold it and on which device. The device code is also at the till, at the bottom left.

## Refunds

A refund is made at the till: the customer's <Ui>Previous Purchases</Ui> → <Ui>Refund</Ui>. More in [Past purchases](/till/past-purchases).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The device is “Unknown” | The purchase was made with an older till version. Update the till. |
| A purchase is missing | Clear the filters: <Ui>Reset</Ui>. Check the date and the shop. |

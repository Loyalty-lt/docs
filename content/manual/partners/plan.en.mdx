## Plan

Open <Path>Billing → Monthly plan</Path>.

<Shot id="planas" />

You see your plan, its limits and the other plans. To change, press <Ui>Select</Ui> next to a plan. Before confirming you see from when it applies and what it costs.

<Callout type="warn" title="The price changes">
The plan should be changed by whoever is responsible for invoices. If unsure, write a [support ticket](/partners/support).
</Callout>

## Add-ons

<Path>Billing → Services & Pricing</Path>: extras on top of the plan, such as more emails.

<Shot id="paslaugos" />

Once turned on, the fee is added to the monthly invoice. A turned-off add-on stays valid until the end of the month.

## Contract

<Path>Billing → Contract</Path>: the contract number, status and a file to download.

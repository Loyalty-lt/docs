You land here after signing in. You see what matters most today.

<Shot id="skydelis" />

- **Invoices** – whether any are unpaid.
- **Getting started** – what is still worth doing. Done steps are marked. The cross hides the guide.
- **Numbers** – customers, cards, points and the purchase total. Refunds are taken off the total.
- **Charts** – how it changed over the year.

## Ask in your own words

In the <Ui>The report in plain language</Ui> block type a question and press <Ui>Explain with AI</Ui>. The AI uses only this page's numbers.

<Callout title="AI can be wrong">
Check the numbers in a report before making a decision.
</Callout>

## Menu

On the left – all sections of the portal. At the top – search, language, notifications and help. The question mark opens the guide page about what you see.

## Generate

<Steps>
<Step>

Choose the report type, the shop and the dates.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the format and press <Ui>Generate report</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Ui>Quick generate</Ui> at the bottom creates the most common reports with one press.

| Report | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Sales report | Purchases and revenue. Refunds are taken off. |
| Customer activity | Who comes back and who stopped visiting. |
| Points distribution | How many points were given and used. |
| Card usage | How the cards are used. |
| Transaction report | Every purchase on its own. |

Earlier reports are under the <Ui>Report history</Ui> tab.

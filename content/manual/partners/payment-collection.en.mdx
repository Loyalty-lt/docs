The service lets you take customer payments online. The money goes to your Stripe account; the fee is taken off by itself.

Open <Path>Payment Collection</Path>.

<Shot id="mokejimu-surinkimas" />

## Turn it on

<Steps>
<Step>

At the bottom of the page press <Ui>Create a Stripe account</Ui>. The Stripe website opens.

</Step>
<Step>

Fill in the company details, confirm your identity and enter the bank account. It takes 5–15 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

Come back to the portal. When Stripe approves the account (up to 1–2 working days), turn the service on.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

The <Ui>Pricing</Ui> block shows the prices. The <Ui>Fee calculator</Ui> shows what is left from a given amount.

If you have a discount code, type it on the right and press <Ui>Apply</Ui>.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Complete the Stripe Connect setup first” | The Stripe account is not finished. Continue with the same button. |
| Stripe asks for more documents | Upload them on the Stripe website. Loyalty.lt does not see them. |

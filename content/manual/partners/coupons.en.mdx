A customer wins a coupon in a game (for example, by collecting stamps) or gets it for an offer. Usually the cashier redeems it. Here you see all coupons.

Open <Path>Coupons</Path>.

<Shot id="kuponai-sarasas" />

## Coupon page

In the list press the eye. You see who the coupon belongs to, until when it is valid and who redeemed it.

<Shot id="kuponai-vienas" />

## Redeem a coupon in the portal

Use this when no till is at hand.

<Callout type="warn" title="A redeemed coupon cannot be returned">
Before confirming, make sure the customer really gets the gift now.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Scan and redeem</Ui>.

<Shot id="kuponai-skenuoti" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the shop, type the coupon code and press <Ui>Check coupon</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Look at what the coupon is and press <Ui>Redeem coupon</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Can be redeemed. |
| Used | Already redeemed; you see when and where. |
| Expired | Its validity ended. |
| Cancelled | Can no longer be redeemed. |

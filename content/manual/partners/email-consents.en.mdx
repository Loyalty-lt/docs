## Who we send to

Open <Path>Email messages → Consents</Path>.

<Shot id="pastas-sutikimai" />

- **Only those who consented** – the safest choice.
- **Everyone who has not opted out** – a larger audience, but the legal basis is your responsibility.

The customer gives consent in the app, at the till or on a website. Consent ticked at the till is confirmed by the customer through an email.

## We no longer email

The <Ui>We no longer email</Ui> list has those who opted out, whose address does not exist and who complained. They get no emails.

## Sent emails

<Path>Email messages → Emails</Path> shows every email: to whom, when, whether it was opened.

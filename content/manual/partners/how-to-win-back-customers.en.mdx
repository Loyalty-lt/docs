---
title: Win customers back
description: How to write to those who have not visited for a long time, once or all the time.
icon: UserRoundCheck
---

**When:** you notice that some customers do not come back. **Time needed:** 15 minutes.

## Once

<Steps>
<Step>

<Path>Email messages → Audiences</Path>: create an audience with <Ui>Inactive for at least (days)</Ui> – 60.

</Step>
<Step>

<Path>Email messages → Campaigns</Path>: create a campaign for this audience.

</Step>
<Step>

Send a test email to yourself, then to everyone.

</Step>
</Steps>

## All the time

Create a rule: [push](/partners/push-automation), [SMS](/partners/sms-automation) or [email](/partners/email-automation).

<Callout title="One channel">
Start with one channel. The same reminder through three channels is annoying.
</Callout>

Customers see the catalogue in the Loyalty.lt app. The cashier picks from it too if the shop uses “Full POS”.

Open <Path>Products and services → All products and services</Path>.

<Shot id="prekes-sarasas" />

## Add a product

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add product</Ui>, type the name and description and upload a picture. <Ui>Write with AI</Ui> helps with the text.

<Shot id="prekes-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Mark whether it is a product or a service and choose the [purchase part](/partners/loyalty-tiers#purchase-parts). Points and discounts depend on it.

</Step>
<Step>

Enter the price and choose a category. Press <Ui>Create</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Your own or a shared product">
Tick “This is our company's individual product” if you make the product yourself. You cannot change the description of shared products (such as a phone).
</Callout>

## Product page

<Shot id="prekes-viena" />

The <Ui>Offers</Ui> tab has prices and discounts, <Ui>Statistics</Ui> – how many times the product was viewed and clicked.

## Upload many products

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Import</Ui>, then <Ui>Download XML Template</Ui>.

<Shot id="prekes-importas" />

</Step>
<Step>

Fill in the template. Products with the same SKU are updated, not added twice.

</Step>
<Step>

Upload the file (up to 10 MB) and press <Ui>Import Products</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Save products to a file

Press <Ui>Export</Ui>, choose XML, CSV or JSON and press <Ui>Start Export</Ui>. Only your products and offers are saved.

<Shot id="prekes-eksportas" />

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Invalid file type. Please select an XML file.” | Excel cannot be uploaded here – fill in the XML template. |
| The product is not at the till | Check that it is active and assigned to the shop. |

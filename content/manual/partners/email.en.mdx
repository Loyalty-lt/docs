## Create a campaign

Press <Ui>Create Campaign</Ui>. A campaign is a list of steps. A finished step is marked green.

<Shot id="pastas-nauja" />

<Steps>
<Step>

**To** – all customers, a saved [audience](/partners/email-templates) or a filter. You see the number of recipients.

</Step>
<Step>

**From** – the sender name and the email for replies.

</Step>
<Step>

**Subject** – what the customer sees in the inbox. You can test two subjects (A/B).

</Step>
<Step>

**Content** – the email's design. Start from a template or build it from blocks.

</Step>
<Step>

Send a test email to yourself. If all is well – <Ui>Send now</Ui> or set a time.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="A sent email cannot be recalled">
<Ui>Finish later</Ui> saves an unfinished campaign.
</Callout>

## Results

Open a sent campaign. You see how many people opened the email and what they clicked.

<Shot id="pastas-viena" />

<Ui>Send to non-openers</Ui> creates a follow-up for those who did not open the first one.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 0 recipients | Check who agreed to get emails: [Consents](/partners/email-consents). |
| Emails land in spam | Verify [your domain](/partners/email-domains). |

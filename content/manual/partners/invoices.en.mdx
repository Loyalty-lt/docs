## Invoice page

In the list press the eye. You see what it is for, the due date and the lines.

<Shot id="saskaitos-viena" />

<Ui>Download</Ui> saves a PDF. You also get the invoice by email.

## Unpaid

<Path>Billing → Pending Payments</Path> shows what needs paying and what is still accumulating.

<Shot id="saskaitos-laukia" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Late payment interest applies when overdue">
Late payment interest is a separate line on the invoice, with the rate and the number of days.
</Callout>

## If the account is suspended

When invoices stay unpaid, Loyalty.lt can suspend the account. Then:

- only the invoices and the profile open in the portal, and a red banner at the top shows the amount due and the unpaid invoices;
- the till and the shop plugins stop working, and customers no longer see your offers or get notifications in the app.

Click <Ui>Pay</Ui> next to each invoice in the banner. Once every invoice is paid, the account is reactivated automatically – there is nothing to write to us.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You paid, but the invoice shows unpaid | A bank transfer sometimes takes 1–2 days. If it does not update, write a support ticket. |
| You do not get invoices by email | Check <Ui>Billing email</Ui> on the [Company information](/partners/company) page. |

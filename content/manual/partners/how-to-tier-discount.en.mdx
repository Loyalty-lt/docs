---
title: A tier with a discount
description: How to create a tier whose customers get a discount, but not on gift vouchers.
icon: Medal
---

**When:** you want loyal customers to pay less, but not on gift vouchers and promotions. **Time needed:** 10 minutes.

<Steps>
<Step>

<Path>Loyalty tiers</Path>: choose the metric, such as <Ui>Amount spent (€)</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add tier</Ui>: type the name, the threshold (such as 300) and the discount (such as 3 %).

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add part</Ui> and create a part “Gift voucher”: turn off <Ui>Apply the tier discount</Ui> and <Ui>Can be paid with points</Ui>, points multiplier 0.

</Step>
<Step>

Do the same for the “Promotions, memberships” part.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the default part – the one you sell most often.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

At the till, choose a customer of that tier and enter an amount. You see the discount on the right. When you change the part to “Gift voucher”, the discount must disappear.

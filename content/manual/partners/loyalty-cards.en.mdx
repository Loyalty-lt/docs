The card decides how many points a customer gets and what they are worth. The customer sees it in the Loyalty.lt app.

Open <Path>Loyalty Cards → All Cards</Path>.

<Shot id="korteles-sarasas" />

## Create a card

Press <Ui>Add a new loyalty card</Ui>. The form has 5 tabs; on the right you see at once how the card will look.

<Steps>
<Step>

**General.** Type the name in Lithuanian and English. Other languages open under <Ui>Translations</Ui>.

<Shot id="korteles-bendra" />

</Step>
<Step>

**Design.** Upload a logo or a background picture and choose the colours.

<Shot id="korteles-dizainas" />

</Step>
<Step>

**Points.** Set what points are given for and how to spend them.

<Shot id="korteles-taskai" />

</Step>
<Step>

**Shops.** Keep all, or choose only some.

</Step>
<Step>

**Terms.** Write the programme's rules. The customer sees them next to the card. Finally press <Ui>Create</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="How to check">
The “Points simulation” block on the right shows how many points a customer would get for a sample purchase. <Ui>Refresh</Ui> picks another amount.
</Callout>

### Points settings

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Initial bonus points | The customer gets them once, when their card appears. |
| Points expiration (days) | After this many days, unused points expire. Empty – they never expire. |
| Minimum and maximum points | Limits for one purchase. Empty – no limit. |
| Enable points redemption | The customer can pay part of a purchase with points. |
| At most this % paid with points | The largest share of a purchase that points can cover. |

If different goods need different rules, use [purchase parts](/partners/loyalty-tiers#purchase-parts).

## View a card

In the list press the eye. You see how many customers have the card and how many points were given and used.

<Shot id="korteles-viena" />

<Ui>View QR code</Ui> opens a code you can print. A customer scans it with the app and the card appears for them.

## Change a card

In the list press the pencil, change and press <Ui>Save</Ui>.

<Callout type="warn" title="Applies at once">
A new points rate applies to the next purchase. Points already given do not change.
</Callout>

The switch at the bottom of the card turns it off. Customers cannot use a card that is off, but the data stays.

## Delete a card

<Callout type="error" title="Cannot be undone">
A deleted card cannot be restored. If it is only temporarily not needed, turn it off with the switch.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The till gives no points | Check that the card is active, earning is on and the shop is assigned to the card. |
| The till does not let customers pay with points | Check <Ui>Enable points redemption</Ui>, the minimum points and the largest share. |
| A customer does not see the card | <Ui>Assign to new customers automatically</Ui> must be ticked, or the customer must scan the QR code. |

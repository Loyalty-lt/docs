Here you see everyone who has your loyalty card.

Open <Path>Customers</Path>.

<Shot id="klientai-sarasas" />

## Find customers

Search by name, email or phone. Filters narrow the list by tier, points and joining date.

<Ui>Find customers in plain words</Ui> lets you write what you are looking for, such as “no visit for 60 days and over 500 points”. AI turns it into filters – check them before using.

## Customer page

In the list press the eye.

<Shot id="klientai-vienas" />

## Change a customer's details

<Callout type="warn" title="Changes the customer's account too">
Changes apply to the customer's account and the Loyalty.lt app. Change only what the customer asked for.
</Callout>

Press <Ui>Edit</Ui>, change and save.

<Shot id="klientai-redaguoti" />

The <Ui>Check contacts</Ui> badge in the list means the phone number is wrong or there is no contact.

## Assign a tier manually

Press <Ui>Assign tier</Ui>, choose the tier and, if needed, until when it is valid.

<Shot id="klientai-lygis" />

A manual tier applies instead of the automatic one. <Ui>Remove manual assignment</Ui> cancels it.

## Consents

The <Ui>Consents</Ui> block shows whether the customer agreed to your SMS and emails. Turning a channel off stops your campaigns to them.

<Callout type="warn" title="The consent is the customer's">
Turn it on only when the customer really agreed. If they opted out themselves, only they can give it back.
</Callout>

## Save the list

Press <Ui>Export</Ui> and choose CSV or Excel. The customers you see with the current filters are saved.

<Callout type="warn" title="Personal data">
The file has names, phone numbers and email addresses. Keep it safe and delete it when no longer needed.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The customer is not in the list | They do not have your card yet. Register them [at the till](/till/customer). |
| “Withdrawn by the customer – only they can give it again” | Ask the customer to agree in the app. |
| The customer does not see points in the app | They probably signed in with another phone number or email. They must sign in with the number given at the till. |

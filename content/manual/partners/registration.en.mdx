## Submit an application

<Steps>
<Step>

**Company information.** Find your company by name – the register's details fill in by themselves.

</Step>
<Step>

**Contact information.** Enter the contact person and the person who can sign the contract.

</Step>
<Step>

**Business and additional information.** Tell us what you do and what you expect.

</Step>
<Step>

**Plan.** Choose a plan. It decides how many shops, customers and messages the price includes.

</Step>
<Step>

**Review and confirmation.** Check, tick the consents and press <Ui>Submit application</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Follow the status

After submitting you get a tracking code and a link. There you see what is done and can send us a message.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted | We have it and will review it soon. |
| More information needed | Write or upload what is asked. |
| Contract sent | Sign the contract. |
| Awaiting payment | Pay the first invoice. |
| Active | You can sign in to the portal. |

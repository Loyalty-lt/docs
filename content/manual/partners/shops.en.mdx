Here you manage all your locations: what customers see about them and how the till works there.

Open <Path>Shops → All Shops</Path>.

<Shot id="parduotuves-sarasas" />

## Add a shop

<Callout type="warn" title="May cost extra">
How many shops the price includes depends on your plan. The portal warns you when you go over.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add shop</Ui> and type the name, address and contacts.

<Shot id="parduotuves-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Set the opening hours. Customers see them in the app.

<Shot id="parduotuves-darbo-laikas" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose which till the staff will see and press <Ui>Create shop</Ui>.

<Shot id="parduotuves-pos" />

</Step>
</Steps>

You can change the till type later.

## Change a shop

In the list press the pencil, change the fields and press <Ui>Save changes</Ui>. The fields are the same as when creating.

## View a shop

In the list press the eye. On the left you see what customers know about the shop.

<Shot id="parduotuves-viena" />

## Till quick keys and lock

This block is at the bottom of the shop's page. The settings apply to this shop only.

<Shot id="parduotuves-kasa" />

- **Quick keys** – the cashier enters an amount with one press. The arrows change their order.
- **Till lock** – after the set time without activity the till asks for the PIN. This way nobody works under someone else's name.

After changing, press <Ui>Save</Ui>. The button appears only once something has changed.

## Upload many shops from Excel

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Import from Excel</Ui>, then <Ui>Download template</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Fill in the template. Leave the ID empty for a new shop; to change an existing one, enter its ID.

</Step>
<Step>

Upload the file and press <Ui>Upload and import</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot id="parduotuves-importas" />

## Delete a shop

<Callout type="error" title="Cannot be undone">
A deleted shop cannot be restored. If it is only closed for a while, edit it and untick “Shop is active”.
</Callout>

In the list press the bin and confirm.

<Shot id="parduotuves-istrinti" />

The shop's purchases stay under <Ui>Transactions</Ui>, only without the shop's name.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Plan location limit exceeded” | The plan allows fewer shops. Change the plan or turn off the limit on extra quantities. |
| “Enter a label and an amount for every key” | Fill in the empty row or delete it. |
| “Please upload a valid Excel file (.xlsx or .xls)” | Save the file in Excel format, not CSV. |
| New keys do not show at the till | At the till press the refresh arrow at the top. |

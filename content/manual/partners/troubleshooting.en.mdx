## Customers and points

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A customer does not see points in the app | They signed in with another number or email. They must sign in with the number given at the till. |
| A customer did not get the SMS with the app link | The SMS goes only to brand-new customers. You can tell them the link: loyaltylt.app. |
| The till gives no points | See [cards](/partners/loyalty-cards) and [purchase parts](/partners/loyalty-tiers#purchase-parts). |
| A customer did not move up a tier | See [tiers](/partners/loyalty-tiers). |

## Till

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A new discount or key is not at the till | At the till press the refresh arrow. |
| A staff member cannot sign in | Check that they are active and assigned to the shop: [Staff](/partners/staff). |
| A forgotten PIN | On the staff member's page press <Ui>Reset PIN</Ui>. |
| Other till problems | [Till troubleshooting](/till/troubleshooting). |

## Campaigns

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The SMS is waiting for approval | We usually approve within 5 minutes. |
| Few recipients | Promotions reach only those who agreed: [SMS](/partners/sms-consents), [email](/partners/email-consents). |
| Emails land in spam | Verify [your domain](/partners/email-domains). |

## Invoices

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The amount is higher than the plan's price | See what went over: [Billing](/partners/billing). |
| A paid invoice shows unpaid | A transfer sometimes takes 1–2 days. |

## Did not find it?

Write a [support ticket](/partners/support). Say what you did, what you saw and when. Attach a screenshot.

## Consents

Without consent, promotional SMS cannot be sent. The customer gives consent in the app or at the till.

Open <Path>SMS Marketing → Consents</Path>.

<Shot id="sms-sutikimai" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Add only real consents">
By adding or importing numbers you confirm that the customers really agreed. Those who opted out must not get messages.
</Callout>

## Sent messages

<Path>SMS Marketing → Messages</Path> shows every SMS: to whom, when, whether it was delivered and what it cost.

<Shot id="sms-zinutes" />

## Analytics

<Path>SMS Marketing → Analytics</Path> shows delivery, spending and how the campaigns did.

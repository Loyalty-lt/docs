Open <Path>Settings</Path>.

<Shot id="nustatymai" />

| Tab | What you change |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Name, phone, password. |
| General | Language and region. |
| Notifications | Which channels we notify you by. Plan limit warnings. |
| Security | Sign-in notifications and when to sign out. |
| Display | Light or dark, menu size. |
| Advanced | Data and deleting the account. |

## Plan limits

When you reach 80 % or 100 % of a plan limit, we notify you through the chosen channels. Going over does not stop the service; the excess goes on the invoice.

If you do not want to go over, turn on the hard limit in the <Ui>Notifications</Ui> tab.

<Callout type="error" title="Deleting the account cannot be undone">
Deleting the account removes all the data. If you only want to pause the service, write a [support ticket](/partners/support).
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The password does not change | Type the current password. If you do not remember it, sign out and [reset it](/partners/sign-in). |
| The email cannot be changed | Write a support ticket – we will change it. |

The discounts created here appear as buttons next to the amount at the till. For each you set how much, for whom, when and on what conditions.

Open <Path>Till discounts</Path>.

<Shot id="kasos-nuolaidos-sarasas" />

## Create a discount

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add discount</Ui> and choose an example or <Ui>Empty discount</Ui>.

<Shot id="kasos-nuolaidos-pavyzdziai" />

</Step>
<Step>

Type the name and the size. Everything else only if the discount needs conditions.

<Shot id="kasos-nuolaidos-forma" />

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Save</Ui>. At the till the discount appears after refreshing.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Conditions

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Discount | Percent or euros. You set the size here, or let the cashier enter it up to a limit. |
| Applies to | Shops, purchase parts, customer tier. Nothing ticked – applies to all. |
| When valid | Dates, weekdays, hours. At other times the discount does not show at the till. |
| Combining with the loyalty programme | Whether the tier discount also applies, points are earned, points can pay. |
| At the till | Whether it applies by itself, needs a reason or a manager's approval. |

<Callout title="When a manager's PIN is needed">
With <Ui>Approved by a manager</Ui> ticked, a manager enters their PIN at the till. The reason and the approving person stay on the purchase page.
</Callout>

## How much it is used

Next to each discount you see how many times it was applied in 30 days and the total. A discount that is off does not show at the till but stays in the list.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The discount is not at the till | Check that it is on and the shop, day and hour fit. At the till press the refresh arrow. |
| “Enter both hours: from and until” | The time needs both a start and an end. |
| “The end date is before the start” | Swap the dates. |

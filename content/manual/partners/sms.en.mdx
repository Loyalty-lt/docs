An SMS reaches everyone whose phone number you have and who agreed. No app is needed.

Open <Path>SMS Marketing → Campaigns</Path>.

<Shot id="sms-kampanijos" />

If you see a request form instead of campaigns, the SMS service is not on yet. Fill in the form – we will get in touch.

## Send a campaign

<Callout type="warn" title="Costs money and cannot be undone">
You pay for every part of a message. Sent messages cannot be taken back. Send only to those who agreed.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Create Campaign</Ui>. Choose the sender name and write the text.

<Shot id="sms-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the recipients: the button with the number of consents takes those who agreed.

</Step>
<Step>

Check the <Ui>Cost Estimate</Ui> and press <Ui>Submit for Approval</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

Loyalty.lt reviews the campaign – usually within 5 minutes. An approved campaign is sent at once or at the set time.

### How many parts a message takes

- Without Lithuanian letters, one part is 160 characters.
- With Lithuanian letters (ą, č, ę, ė, į, š, ų, ū, ž) – 70 characters.
- A longer message is sent in several parts. You pay for each.

We add the opt-out link to the message ourselves. You do not pay for it.

## Results

Open the campaign. You see how many messages were delivered, how many failed and what it cost. The <Ui>Recipients</Ui> tab lists every number.

<Shot id="sms-viena" />

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “No approved sender names” | First [register a sender name](/partners/sms-templates). |
| Approval <Ui>Rejected</Ui> | Open the campaign, read the reason, fix it and submit again. |
| The message was not delivered | The number is wrong or switched off. Fix the customer's contacts. |

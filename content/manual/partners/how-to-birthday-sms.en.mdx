---
title: Birthday SMS
description: How to greet a customer on their birthday automatically and give points.
icon: Cake
---

**When:** you want a customer to get a message on their birthday. **Needed:** an approved sender name. **Time needed:** 10 minutes.

<Callout type="warn" title="Costs money">
The rule sends SMS every day until you turn it off.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Create a [template](/partners/sms-templates). A message without Lithuanian letters is cheaper.

</Step>
<Step>

<Path>SMS Marketing → Automation</Path>: press <Ui>Create Rule</Ui> and choose the birthday trigger.

</Step>
<Step>

Set the hours, such as 10:00–12:00, and turn on <Ui>Activate immediately</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Save rule</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

The next day the [SMS messages](/partners/sms-consents) page shows the sent messages. Only customers who agreed and have a birth date get them.

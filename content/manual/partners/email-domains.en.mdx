Until the domain is verified, emails are sent from a Loyalty.lt address with your name. Once verified, you send from your own, such as `info@jusu-domenas.lt`.

Open <Path>Email messages → Sender domains</Path>.

<Shot id="pastas-domenai" />

## Add a domain

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add domain</Ui>, type the domain, the sender email and the name.

</Step>
<Step>

Copy the DNS records shown into your domain's control panel. If you do not manage it yourself, forward them to whoever does.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Verify</Ui>. DNS changes sometimes take a few hours.

</Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Still not verified | Wait a few hours and check again. The records must match exactly. |
| Emails go from the Loyalty.lt address again | The domain was deleted or its records removed. Add them again. |

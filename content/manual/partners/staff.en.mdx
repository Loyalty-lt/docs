Everyone who works at the till has their own staff record. That way a purchase shows who sold it.

Open <Path>Staff → All Staff</Path>.

<Shot id="darbuotojai-sarasas" />

## Add a staff member

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add staff member</Ui>, type the name, email and position.

<Shot id="darbuotojai-naujas" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the role.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Staff member | Work at the till. |
| Manager | Work at the till, approve discounts with their PIN and use the portal. |

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Create</Ui>. Then assign shops and permissions on the staff member's page.

</Step>
<Step>

Give the staff member their code: <Ui>Generate Card</Ui> creates a card with the code, <Ui>Send welcome email</Ui> sends it by email.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="The code is like a key">
Anyone with the code can sign in to the till. Do not stick it where it can be seen. If it is lost, generate a new one.
</Callout>

Many staff at once: tick them and press <Ui>A4 QR sheet</Ui> – you print the codes on one sheet.

## Staff member page

In the list press the eye.

<Shot id="darbuotojai-vienas" />

- **Overview** – details, the till PIN and the devices it works on.
- **Permissions** – what they see and can change in the portal.
- **Assigned shops** – where they can work. Nothing chosen – everywhere.
- **Sign-in history** – when, how and on which device they signed in.

## Till PIN

The staff member creates the PIN at the till, the first time they sign in with the code. The portal only shows whether it exists.

If the PIN is forgotten or someone else learned it, press <Ui>Reset PIN</Ui>. Next time the staff member signs in with the code and creates a new PIN.

## Sign-in history

<Shot id="darbuotojas-prisijungimai" />

Failed attempts are shown too. The device code is the same as in the till's corner and on the [Till devices](/partners/till-devices) page.

## A staff member left

Turn the staff member off or delete them.

<Callout type="error" title="Deleting cannot be undone">
Earlier purchases stay. If they may come back, only turn them off.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The staff member cannot sign in | Check that they are active and assigned to that shop. |
| The PIN is locked until a time | Too many wrong attempts. Wait or reset the PIN. |
| No device in the sign-in history | These are old sign-ins. New ones record the device. |

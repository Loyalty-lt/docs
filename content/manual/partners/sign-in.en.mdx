The portal is at [partners.loyalty.lt](https://partners.loyalty.lt). You sign in with your email and password.

<Shot id="prisijungimas" />

Cashiers do not sign in to the portal – they use the [till app](/till/sign-in).

## Forgot the password

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> and type your email.

<Shot id="prisijungimas-slaptazodis" />

</Step>
<Step>

Open the email and press the link. It is valid for 24 hours.

</Step>
<Step>

Type the new password twice. It must be at least 8 characters long.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Confidentiality pledge

If the portal asks, sign that you will protect your customers' data. You confirm your identity with a one-time code and draw your signature. Until you sign, you do not see customer data.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Invalid email or password” | Check that Caps Lock is off. Reset the password. |
| No email arrives | Look in the spam folder. Make sure it is the same email address. |
| “The link has expired or has already been used” | Ask for a new link. |
| “Security check failed. Please try again.” | Reload the page. Turn off ad blocking for this site. |

We use these details on invoices; customers see the name and the logo.

Open <Path>Company Information</Path>.

<Shot id="imone-duomenys" />

## Tabs

- **Company details** – names, codes, address and contacts.
- **Bank details** – the bank and the account number.
- **Brand** – the logo and the colours. They colour what customers see in the app.

<Shot id="imone-zenklas" />

After changing, press <Ui>Save</Ui>.

<Callout title="Two names">
The “Display name” is what customers see. The “Legal name” is what goes on invoices.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices go to the wrong person | Fill in <Ui>Billing email</Ui>. If it is empty, the contact email is used. |
| The colour is not saved | Write it with a hash and 6 characters, such as `#4D903F`. |

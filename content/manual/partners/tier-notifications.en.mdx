A rule sends the customer a notification when their tier changes. You can send push, email and SMS – the channels your plan includes.

Open <Path>Loyalty tiers</Path> and press <Ui>Manage notifications</Ui>.

<Shot id="lygiu-pranesimai-sarasas" />

## Create a rule

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add notification</Ui> and choose the event. If needed, choose one tier.

<Shot id="lygiu-pranesimai-naujas" />

</Step>
<Step>

Tick the channels and write the title and text. Click a variable to insert it, such as `{{tier}}` – the tier's name.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Save rule</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="SMS costs money">
You pay for every SMS at your rates. Push and emails count towards your plan.
</Callout>

## Events

| Event | When it is sent |
| --- | --- |
| Tier upgraded | The customer reached a higher tier. |
| Next tier approaching | The customer has gone the set share of the way, such as 80 %. |
| Grace ending | The tier will drop soon. |
| Tier downgraded | The customer went back to a lower tier. |
| Tier assigned manually | You changed the tier on the customer's page. |

## Test it

In the list press <Ui>Send me a test</Ui>. The test notification comes to you, not to customers.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Your plan includes no notification channels.” | Change the plan or turn the service on. |
| The customer did not get it | Push reaches only those with the app. SMS and email need a phone or an email address. |

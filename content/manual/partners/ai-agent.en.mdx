With a key, your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others) can work in the portal through chat: find a customer, prepare a campaign, create a discount.

Open <Path>AI agent keys</Path>.

<Shot id="ai-raktai" />

## Connect

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>New key</Ui> and name the assistant it is for.

</Step>
<Step>

Copy the key – we will not show it again.

</Step>
<Step>

On the page choose your assistant and follow the steps. Put your key in place of `lmcp_…`.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="The key can do what you can">
The agent can change prices, send campaigns and see customers. Keep the key like a password. Revoke the ones you no longer need.
</Callout>

Only the account owner creates and revokes keys.

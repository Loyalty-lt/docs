## Sender name

The customer sees the sender name instead of a number. Without an approved name a campaign cannot be sent.

Open <Path>SMS Marketing → Sender Names</Path>.

<Shot id="sms-vardai" />

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add Sender Name</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Type up to 11 characters: letters, digits, dot, hyphen, space. The name cannot be changed later.

</Step>
<Step>

Wait for our approval. The name must match your company or brand.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Templates

A template is a ready text for a campaign or an automatic message.

Open <Path>SMS Marketing → Templates</Path>.

<Shot id="sms-sablonai" />

Press <Ui>Create Template</Ui>, type the name, category and text. You can insert the customer's name into the text.

<Shot id="sms-sablonas" />

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The name is rejected | Choose a name close to your company's name. Other brands cannot be used. |

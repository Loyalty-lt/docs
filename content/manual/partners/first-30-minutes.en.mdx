These steps are the least you need to serve your first customer.

<Steps>
<Step>

**Company.** Enter your details and upload the logo: [Company information](/partners/company).

</Step>
<Step>

**Shop.** Add the address, opening hours and till type: [Shops](/partners/shops).

</Step>
<Step>

**Card.** Set how many points per euro and what they are worth: [Loyalty cards](/partners/loyalty-cards).

</Step>
<Step>

**Staff.** Add the cashiers and give them their codes: [Staff](/partners/staff).

</Step>
<Step>

**Till.** Install the app and sign in with a code: [Installing](/till/install).

</Step>
<Step>

**Test.** At the till, register yourself with your phone and make a €1 purchase. It appears under [Transactions](/partners/transactions).

</Step>
</Steps>

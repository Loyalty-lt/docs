## Templates

A template is an email design you use many times.

Open <Path>Email messages → Templates</Path>.

<Shot id="pastas-sablonai" />

<Ui>Create template</Ui> opens the builder. You cannot change shared templates, but you can use them.

## Audiences

An audience is a saved filter, such as “no visit for 60 days”. The recipients are chosen anew every time.

Open <Path>Email messages → Audiences</Path>.

<Shot id="pastas-auditorijos" />

Press <Ui>Create audience</Ui>. You can filter by tier, points, joining date, days inactive and whether a campaign was left unopened.

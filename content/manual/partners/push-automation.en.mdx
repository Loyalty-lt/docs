A rule sends a notification by itself when a customer meets the condition.

Open <Path>Push Notifications → Automation</Path>.

<Shot id="push-taisykles" />

## Create a rule

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Add Rule</Ui>, type the name and the text. AI can suggest the text.

<Shot id="push-taisykle" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose when to send, for example when a customer has not visited for a number of days.

</Step>
<Step>

Set the time, the days and how often for the same person. Press <Ui>Save rule</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="So it does not look like spam">
<Ui>Cooldown (hours)</Ui> stops sending too often. <Ui>Max sends per user</Ui> limits how many times one person gets the same one.
</Callout>

## Stop a rule

Open the rule and turn off <Ui>Activate immediately</Ui> or change its status.

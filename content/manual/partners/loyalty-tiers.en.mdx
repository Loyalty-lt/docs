Tiers make customers come back: the more they buy, the more they get. Here you also set what a purchase consists of.

Open <Path>Loyalty tiers</Path>.

<Shot id="lygiai-puslapis" />

## Start from a template

Press <Ui>Templates</Ui>, look through them and press <Ui>Use</Ui>.

<Callout type="warn" title="Replaces your current tiers">
Applying a template replaces the current tiers with the template's tiers.
</Callout>

## Programme settings

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Metric | What moves a customer up: points earned, amount spent or visits. |
| Period | Over what time it counts: always, 3, 6 or 12 months, or a calendar year. |
| Allow downgrade | When on, a customer can drop. When off, a reached tier stays for good. |
| Grace (days) | How many days a tier holds after falling below the threshold. |

Changes are saved at once.

## Add a tier

Press <Ui>Add tier</Ui> and fill in the window.

<Shot id="lygiai-lygis" />

The <Ui>Formula</Ui> switch lets you write a condition instead of a number. If the formula fails, the simple value applies. AI can write the formula – describe the rule in your own words and check it with <Ui>Test</Ui>.

The arrows in the list change the order of tiers. The bin deletes a tier.

## Purchase parts

Parts let you apply different rules, for example: a tier discount only on cosmetics, and no points for a gift voucher. The cashier chooses what each amount is for.

<Shot id="lygiai-dalys" />

Press <Ui>Add part</Ui> or the pencil.

<Shot id="lygiai-dalis" />

- **Points multiplier** – 0 means “no points”, 1 – as usual, 2 – double.
- **Apply the tier discount** – whether the customer's tier discount applies to this part. Below you can set another percentage for a single tier.
- **Can be paid with points** – whether the customer can cover this part with points.
- **Counts towards tier progress** – whether this amount moves the customer up.
- **Default part** – applies when the cashier does not choose one.

<Callout title="Promotions and memberships">
Create a part “Promotions, memberships”: multiplier 0, no tier discount, no paying with points. Then a promotion does not stack with the loyalty discount.
</Callout>

## Assign a tier manually

Open the customer's page and press <Ui>Assign tier</Ui>. More in [Customers](/partners/customers).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The customer should have moved up but did not | Tiers are recalculated after a purchase and once a day. Check that the part counts towards tier progress. |
| No tier discount at the till | Check that the purchase part allows it and the programme is on. |
| “The formula must return a number.” | The formula's result must be a number, such as `amount > 50 ? 2 : 1`. |

---
title: A new staff member at the till
description: From the record in the portal to the first sale with a PIN.
icon: UserPlus
---

**Time needed:** 5 minutes.

<Steps>
<Step>

In the portal: <Path>Staff</Path> → <Ui>Add staff member</Ui>. Type the name, email and role.

</Step>
<Step>

Assign a shop and press <Ui>Generate Card</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

At the till: the staff member scans the code and creates a 4-digit PIN. Next time the PIN is enough.

</Step>
<Step>

Give them the [one-page guide](/till/new-cashier) to read.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

On the staff member's page the <Ui>Login history</Ui> tab shows the sign-in with the code, and <Ui>Till PIN</Ui> shows <Ui>Set</Ui>.

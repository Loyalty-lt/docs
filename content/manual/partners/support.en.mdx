Here you report a problem or ask for help.

Open <Path>Support → All Tickets</Path>.

<Shot id="uzklausos-sarasas" />

## Write to us

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>New Ticket</Ui>.

<Shot id="uzklausos-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Type the subject and the description: what you did, what you saw, when. Attach a screenshot.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the type and the priority, press <Ui>Submit Ticket</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="When to choose a high priority">
When you cannot sell or the till does not work. Each priority shows how soon we start working on it.
</Callout>

## The answer

Open the ticket. Here you see the conversation and can add a comment. You get a notification about our answer.

<Shot id="uzklausos-viena" />

## Tickets by email

Under <Ui>Email Settings</Ui> you link your address. Then an email sent to the address shown there becomes a ticket by itself.

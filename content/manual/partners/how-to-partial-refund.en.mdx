---
title: Refund part of a purchase
description: How to give a customer part of the money back and what happens to the points.
icon: Undo2
---

**When:** the customer returns only part. **Where:** at the till.

<Steps>
<Step>

At the till choose the customer and press <Ui>Previous Purchases</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Next to the purchase press <Ui>Refund</Ui> and enter the amount. More in [Past purchases](/till/past-purchases).

</Step>
</Steps>

Points earned on the refunded amount are taken back, and the part paid with points goes back to the customer.

## Check that it worked

In the portal, on the [Transactions](/partners/transactions) page, a row with a minus appears. The turnover drops by the refunded amount.

A push notification appears on the customer's phone if they have the Loyalty.lt app and allowed notifications.

Open <Path>Push Notifications → Campaigns</Path>.

<Shot id="push-kampanijos" />

## Send a campaign

<Callout type="warn" title="A sent notification cannot be recalled">
Read the text once more before sending. If unsure, save a draft.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Create Campaign</Ui> and fill in the form.

<Shot id="push-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose who to send to. On the right you see how many customers will get it.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Send Campaign</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Results

In the list press the campaign. You see how many recipients there were and how many got it.

<Shot id="push-viena" />

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer recipients than customers | Only those who installed the app and allowed notifications get them. |
| The plan limit is reached | The limit shows next to the recipient count. Wait for next month or change the plan. |

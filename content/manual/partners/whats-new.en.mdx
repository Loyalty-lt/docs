Till changes are [listed separately](/till/whats-new).

## 2026-11-26

- The guide in English – switch the language at the top. The addresses are now in English, such as `/till/customer`; the old ones still work.
- The whole guide as a PDF – the link is on the section's first page.
- In English, the shop form's name field says “Shop name”.

## 2026-11-25

- This guide. The question mark at the top left opens the page about the screen you are on.
- [Till discounts](/partners/till-discounts) with conditions, times and manager approval.
- [Till devices](/partners/till-devices): code, shop, who can sign in with a PIN.
- The staff page shows the till PIN status, <Ui>Reset PIN</Ui> and the sign-in history with the device.
- The purchase page is a breakdown: parts, discounts, points, till, staff member.
- Purchases have a reference number. The customer's page shows their purchases.
- After a purchase the customer gets a notice with the amount, the discount and the points.
- Refunds reduce the turnover on the dashboard, in reports and at the till.
- The shop's page shows real numbers and its purchases.
- The coupons section also shows coupons won in games.
- Our replies are visible again in support tickets.
- In billing, the estimate matches what will be on the invoice.
- The SMS sent to a new customer at the till reminds them to sign in with the same phone number.

## 2026-11-24

- [Purchase parts](/partners/loyalty-tiers#purchase-parts): separate rules for goods, services, promotions and gift vouchers.
- The shop's settings have the till's quick keys and the lock after inactivity.

## 2026-11-19

- An email builder with blocks, templates and campaign steps.
- [Promotions](/partners/promotions) show in the app only after our approval.
- The customer's page shows SMS and email consents.
- All dates follow the chosen format.

## 2026-11-18

- Products and services with a duration. A promotion for a whole category.
- A full customer details form.

## 2026-11-07

- Tiers: 3 and 6 month periods, a point value and a limit on the part paid with points.

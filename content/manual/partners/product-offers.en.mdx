A promotion sets the product's price, discount and the time it is valid. Customers see it next to the product in the app.

Open <Path>Products and services → Product Offers</Path>.

<Shot id="pasiulymai-sarasas" />

## Create a promotion for one product

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Create promotion</Ui> and choose the product.

<Shot id="pasiulymai-nauja" />

</Step>
<Step>

Enter the price and the discounted price, or the discount percentage.

</Step>
<Step>

Set from when until when it is valid, and save.

</Step>
</Steps>

## A promotion for a whole category

<Callout type="warn" title="Changes many products at once">
The discount applies to all products of the chosen category and its subcategories. Products that already have another promotion at that time are skipped.
</Callout>

Press <Ui>Category promotion</Ui>, choose the category, the discount and the dates. The usual price does not change – it comes back by itself when the promotion ends.

<Shot id="pasiulymai-kategorijai" />

## Stop a promotion

In the list press the switch. A hidden promotion is not shown to customers.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “You already have an active offer for this product during the provided dates. Offers cannot overlap in time.” | Change the dates or stop the earlier promotion first. |

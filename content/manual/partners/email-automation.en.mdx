Open <Path>Email messages → Automations</Path>.

<Shot id="pastas-taisykles" />

## Create a rule

Press <Ui>Create rule</Ui>, choose when to send and write the email.

| When | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| N days after joining the programme | A welcome for a new customer. 0 – the same day. |
| Birthday | An email on the birthday. |
| No purchase for N days (bought before) | A reminder about you. |
| Points expire within N days | Encourages using the points. |
| Anniversary of joining the programme | Thanks for staying. |

Emails are sent only between 08:00 and 20:00 Lithuanian time.

<Callout type="warn" title="Run now">
When you run a rule by hand, everyone who meets the condition right now gets the email at once. Look at who they are first.
</Callout>

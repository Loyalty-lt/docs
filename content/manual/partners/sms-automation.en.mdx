A rule sends an SMS by itself when a customer meets the condition.

Open <Path>SMS Marketing → Automation</Path>.

<Shot id="sms-taisykles" />

## Create a rule

<Callout type="warn" title="Every message costs money">
The rule keeps sending until you turn it off. First check how many customers meet the condition.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Create Rule</Ui>, choose the sender name and a template, or write the text.

<Shot id="sms-taisykle" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the trigger – when to send.

</Step>
<Step>

Set the hours and days when sending is allowed. Press <Ui>Save rule</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

Only customers who agreed to your messages get the SMS.

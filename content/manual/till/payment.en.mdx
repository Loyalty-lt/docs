<Callout type="warn" title="Loyalty.lt does not take the money">
The till records the purchase, the discounts and the points. Take the money with your cash register or card terminal.
</Callout>

A purchase needs a customer. Without one, Loyalty.lt does not record it.

## Finish a purchase

<Steps>
<Step>

Check the basket and the amount. Press <Ui>Pay</Ui> or <Key>F12</Key>.

</Step>
<Step>

Take the money and press <Ui>Pay</Ui> again in the window.

<Shot id="kasa-mokejimas" />

</Step>
</Steps>

If “Ask for payment method” is on in the settings, choose cash or card first.

## What the customer gets

In the app the customer sees a notice at once: what they paid, the discount, how many points they got and used, and how many they have now.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Pay</Ui> is grey | The amount is zero or not confirmed. Enter the amount and press <Ui>OK</Ui>. |
| “Pick the customer first” | Scan the card or find the customer and pay again. |
| The purchase is not recorded, another error shows | Check the internet connection and press again. The basket is kept. |
| A wrong amount was recorded | [Refund](/till/past-purchases) the purchase and enter it again. |

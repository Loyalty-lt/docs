## The till does not sign in

| Sign | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| The code does not work | Make sure the bottom says “PRODUCTION”, not the test environment. |
| The PIN is locked | Wait 15 minutes or sign in with your code. [Signing in](/till/sign-in) |
| It asks to choose a shop | Choose the one you work in. |
| An “Account suspended” screen | The partner has to pay the invoices in the portal. Once paid, press “Retry”. [Invoices](/partners/invoices) |

## No internet

Without the internet a purchase cannot be recorded. The amount stays on the screen or is parked. When the connection is back, press <Ui>Pay</Ui>.

## The scanner does not scan

- Check that the scanner works as a keyboard: scan a code into a plain text window.
- The scanner must send <Key>Enter</Key> after the code. That is the factory setting.
- Close open windows and try again.

## The points were not added

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No customer was chosen | Without a customer the purchase is not recorded. Scan the card and enter the purchase. |
| The purchase part earns no points | Your manager set it so. |
| The customer sees an old balance | In the app, pull the list down. |

## The screen looks outdated

In a browser press <Key>Ctrl</Key>+<Key>Shift</Key>+<Key>R</Key> (macOS – <Key>⌘</Key>+<Key>Shift</Key>+<Key>R</Key>).

## When asking for help

Give the device code and the app version – both are at the bottom of the sign-in screen. Write to [info@loyalty.lt](mailto:info@loyalty.lt).

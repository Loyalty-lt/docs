## Find a purchase

Choose the customer and press <Ui>Previous Purchases</Ui>.

<Shot id="kasa-istorija" />

## Refund

<Steps>
<Step>

Next to the purchase, press <Ui>Refund</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Keep ticked only the parts being returned. If not the whole amount is refunded, change it.

<Shot id="kasa-grazinimas" />

</Step>
<Step>

Press the refund button with the amount. Hand the money back yourself.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to the points

- Points earned on the refunded part are taken back.
- Points used on the refunded part are returned to the customer.
- The customer's tier is recalculated at once.

<Callout type="error" title="Cannot be undone">
A refund cannot be cancelled. If you made a mistake, enter the purchase again.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The purchase is already refunded | The whole amount was refunded. |
| The amount does not match the purchase | Enter no more than is left to refund. |
| The purchase is not in the list | Another customer is chosen. |

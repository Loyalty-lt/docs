The till updates itself: [Updates](/till/updates). You see your version in <Path>Settings → App Version</Path>.

## 1.6.16 – 2026-11-26

- The till is fully in English: before, some buttons (for example in the printer window) showed internal names.
- Help opens the guide in the till's language.

## 1.6.15 – 2026-11-25

- A help link on the sign-in screen and in the settings opens this guide.

## 1.6.14 – 2026-11-25

- Paying without a customer now says “Pick the customer first” instead of an unclear error.
- “Previous Purchases” show the local time.
- The coupon window shows until when the coupon is valid.
- The window of a product coupon with a price no longer freezes.
- On the dashboard a refund is no longer shown as a purchase; the monthly bars grow from the bottom.

## 1.6.13 – 2026-11-25

- The customer shows at once, even when the live connection to the server drops.

## 1.6.12 – 2026-11-25

- Every purchase and refund is saved with the till's device code. The portal shows which till sold it.

## 1.6.11 – 2026-11-25

- When registering a new customer you can enter the first and last name and tick consent to SMS and emails.

## 1.6.10 – 2026-11-25

- Customers see only the cashier's first name and initial in the app, such as “Jonas P.”.

## 1.6.9 – 2026-11-25

- Till discounts follow the conditions set in the portal.
- The browser till always opens the newest version.

## 1.6.8 – 2026-11-25

- A discount on the whole amount with one button.
- The customer's phone can be pasted.
- The Windows till updates without any windows and needs no administrator rights.

## 1.6.7 – 2026-11-25

- New versions are offered while you work. macOS updates with one click.

## 1.6.5–1.6.6 – 2026-11-25

- Quick staff switching with a PIN.
- The till shows its device code. With a single shop the till opens it straight away.

## 1.6.3 – 2026-11-24

- Part of a purchase can be refunded – by line or by amount.

## 1.6.0 – 2026-11-24

- A new till look: number pad, quick keys, basket.
- Keyboard shortcuts and parked sales.
- Products and quick keys from the shop's settings.

## 1.5.0 – 2026-11-24

- Purchase parts: each part of the amount has its own rules for points and discounts.

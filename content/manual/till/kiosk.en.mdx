Kiosk mode is for a tablet at the counter: nobody can leave the app without a PIN.

## Android tablet

<Steps>
<Step>

Open <Path>Settings</Path> and quickly tap the <Ui>App Version</Ui> row 10 times.

</Step>
<Step>

Create a kiosk PIN. It is different from a staff PIN.

</Step>
<Step>

Turn on <Ui>Kiosk mode</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

The settings are hidden on purpose, so that a customer does not find them by chance.

In kiosk mode you can:

- connect to WiFi without leaving the app;
- check for updates;
- restart the device.

<Callout>
For the tablet to lock completely, the app must be made the device owner. The command is on the same page. If you see it for the first time, write to us.
</Callout>

## Computer

<Path>Settings → Full screen mode</Path> fills the whole screen without the window frame.

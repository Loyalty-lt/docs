A customer wins a coupon in a game or gets it for an offer. Each coupon can be used once.

## Redeem a coupon

<Steps>
<Step>

Choose the customer and open <Ui>Coupons</Ui>.

<Shot id="kasa-kuponai" />

</Step>
<Step>

Open the coupon, read the terms and press <Ui>Redeem coupon</Ui>.

<Shot id="kasa-kuponas" />

</Step>
</Steps>

If the customer shows the coupon's QR code on their phone, just scan it – the window opens by itself.

<Callout type="warn" title="A redeemed coupon cannot be returned">
Before you press, make sure this customer is buying and the terms are met.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “No coupons available” | The customer has no valid coupons, or no customer is chosen. |
| “Used” or “Expired” | The coupon is no longer valid. |
| The coupon is already waiting for payment | It is already in the purchase. Finish the purchase. |

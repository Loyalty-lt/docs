The till shows the games your manager turned on.

## Stamp card

<Steps>
<Step>

Choose the customer and open <Ui>Games</Ui>.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the stamp card, set the number and press <Ui>Add Stamps</Ui>.

<Shot id="kasa-antspaudai" />

</Step>
</Steps>

When all the stamps are collected, the customer gets the prize at once – a coupon in their app.

## Other games

The wheel of fortune, scratch card, quiz and memory game are played in the customer's app. At the till you can start the ones that allow it.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “No games available” | Choose the customer first. If it is still empty, no games are on for this shop. |

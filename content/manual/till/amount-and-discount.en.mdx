This page is about the “Simple POS”: the cashier enters an amount, not products. Every signed-in staff member can use it.

Open <Path>POS System → Amount</Path>.

<Shot id="kasa-langas" />

## Enter an amount

<Steps>
<Step>

Type the amount with the buttons or the keyboard. Digits start from the cents: <Key>1</Key> <Key>0</Key> <Key>0</Key> <Key>0</Key> <Key>0</Key> is €100.00.

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>OK</Ui> or <Key>Enter</Key>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot id="kasa-suma-surinkta" />

<Callout title="Faster">
Quick keys enter frequent amounts. On a computer press <Key>Alt</Key> and a digit, such as <Key>Alt</Key>+<Key>1</Key>. Your manager sets the keys – see [Shops](/partners/shops#till-quick-keys-and-lock).
</Callout>

## Apply a discount

Apply the discount **before** pressing <Ui>OK</Ui>. Then you see what the customer pays before you choose the purchase part.

<Steps>
<Step>

Enter the amount without the discount.

</Step>
<Step>

Press a discount button, such as <Ui>20% off</Ui>. On a computer – <Key>F6</Key>.

</Step>
<Step>

Check the new amount and press <Ui>OK</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot id="kasa-suma-nuolaida" />

Your manager sets up the discounts. If there are many, some are behind the “More” button. If there are none, you do not see the row.

### When the till asks for something

Some discounts have conditions:

- **Reason.** Type what you checked and press <Ui>Apply discount</Ui>.
- **Customer.** First scan the card or find the customer.
- **Manager's PIN.** The manager chooses themselves and enters their PIN. A manager needs no approval.
- **Size.** Enter a percentage or euros. The till does not accept more than allowed.

<Shot id="kasa-suma-priezastis" />

## Choose what the amount is for

If your programme has purchase parts, after <Ui>OK</Ui> the till asks what the amount is for. Each part shows the amount with its discount and whether it earns points.

<Shot id="kasa-suma-uz-ka" />

Choose with the mouse, with <Key>↑</Key> <Key>↓</Key> and <Key>Enter</Key>, or with the part's number. <Key>Esc</Key> cancels.

## Add several amounts

Enter another amount and press <Ui>OK</Ui> again. It goes into the basket below the first one. Each amount has its own part and its own discount.

<Shot id="kasa-suma-krepselis" />

## On a phone

Everything works the same way; the basket and the customer open with buttons at the bottom.

<Shot id="kasa-suma-telefonas" />

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The discount button is grey | The amount is too small, or a customer is needed first. Choose a customer or raise the amount. |
| “Pick the customer first” | Scan the card or find the customer, then press the discount. |
| “the discount does not apply to this part” | Your manager allowed this discount for other parts. The amount stays without a discount. |
| No manager with a PIN on this till | A manager must sign in here with their code once and create a PIN. |
| The discount is gone | It is valid at another time, or your manager turned it off. |

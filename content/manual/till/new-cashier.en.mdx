Read this page and you can start working. The links go into detail.

## Before you start

Get your **staff code** from your manager – a card with a QR code or an email. You need it only the first time.

<Steps>
<Step>

**Sign in.** Scan your code and create a 4-digit PIN. [More](/till/sign-in)

</Step>
<Step>

**Every day** choose yourself in “Who is working?” and enter your PIN.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Selling

<Steps>
<Step>

Scan the customer's card or search by phone. [Customer](/till/customer)

</Step>
<Step>

Enter the amount and press <Ui>OK</Ui>. Apply a discount before that if needed. [Amount and discount](/till/amount-and-discount)

</Step>
<Step>

If the customer wants, use their [points](/till/points) or [coupon](/till/coupons).

</Step>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Pay</Ui>, take the money and press <Ui>Pay</Ui> again. [Payment](/till/payment)

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Loyalty.lt does not take the money">
The till records the purchase and the points. Take the money with your cash register or card terminal as usual.
</Callout>

## Remember

- **Work under your own name.** When another person takes over, press <Ui>Switch staff</Ui>.
- **Made a mistake?** A purchase can be refunded in full or in part. [Refunds](/till/past-purchases)
- **The customer forgot their wallet?** Park the sale. [Parked sales](/till/parked-sales)
- **Not sure what to press?** On a computer, <Key>F1</Key> shows the shortcuts.

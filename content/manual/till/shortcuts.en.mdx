On a computer you can work without a mouse. <Key>F1</Key> opens the list at any time.

<Shot id="kasa-klavisai" />

## In lists

- <Key>↑</Key> <Key>↓</Key> move the selection, <Key>Enter</Key> confirms, <Key>Esc</Key> closes.
- Choose a purchase part or a staff member by its number.

<Callout title="macOS">
If the F keys change the volume or brightness, press them together with <Key>fn</Key>.
</Callout>

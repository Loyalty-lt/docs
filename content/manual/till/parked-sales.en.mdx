Park a sale when the customer goes back for their wallet or is still choosing. Serve the next customer in the meantime.

## Park and continue

<Steps>
<Step>

Press <Ui>Park</Ui> (<Key>F8</Key>). The till is empty for the next customer.

</Step>
<Step>

Later press the parked sales button with the number, or <Key>F9</Key>, and choose the sale.

<Shot id="kasa-atideti" />

</Step>
</Steps>

Parked sales stay on this till even after a restart. Those older than a day are marked.

<Callout>
A discount approved by a manager is dropped when you continue the sale: its approval is short-lived. Apply it again.
</Callout>

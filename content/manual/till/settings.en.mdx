Open <Path>Settings</Path>. Most settings apply only to this device.

<Shot id="kasa-nustatymai" />

## Fit the till to your work

- **Till control** – for a mouse and keyboard, or for touch. Automatic suits most.
- **On-screen number pad** – hide it if you have a physical keyboard.
- **Large buttons** – easier on a touch screen.
- **Basket on the left** – for left-handed people or a screen set up the other way.

<Callout type="warn" title="Environment">
Under <Ui>Environment</Ui>, production must be chosen. In the test environment real codes and customers do not work.
</Callout>

## Change the language

<Path>Settings → Language</Path>. Lithuanian and English are available.

## First start on a phone or tablet

The app asks for permissions. Only the ones you use are needed:

- **Camera** – to scan cards.
- **Bluetooth** – for a wireless receipt printer.
- **Notifications** – for important events.

## What else you need

- An internet connection.
- A scanner (optional): any scanner that works as a keyboard. Nothing to set up.
- A receipt printer (optional) – see [Printer](/till/printer).

Choose the customer before payment: without a customer the purchase cannot be recorded. The customer gets the points and their tier discount. An amount you have already entered is kept.

## Find a customer

There are three ways:

- **With a scanner or the camera** – scan the QR code or barcode on the card. With a scanner you press nothing.
- **By card number** – <Ui>Manually</Ui> → <Ui>Card</Ui>.
- **By phone** – <Ui>Manually</Ui> → <Ui>Phone</Ui>.

<Callout title="Faster">

</Callout>

## Register a new customer

The window opens in two ways: with <Ui>New</Ui>, or when a phone search finds nobody.

<Steps>
<Step>

Type the phone number. The till checks whether such a customer already exists.

</Step>
<Step>

Type the first and last name. This is optional: the customer can add it in the app.

</Step>
<Step>

Ask whether the customer wants news, and tick the boxes only if they agree.

</Step>
<Step>

If the customer gets a plastic card, type or scan its number. Press <Ui>Register</Ui>.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot id="kasa-klientas-naujas" />

<Callout type="warn" title="The consent is the customer's">
The boxes are never ticked in advance. If the customer once opted out of your SMS, ticking the box does not change that.
</Callout>

A new customer gets an SMS with a link to the Loyalty.lt app right away.

<Callout title="Tell the customer">
In the app they must sign in with the same phone number. Then all the points and coupons from the till are in their account.
</Callout>

### What the till asks

| Which customer | What the till asks |
| --- | --- |
| Completely new | First name, last name, email and consents |
| Has a Loyalty.lt account, first time with you | Only consents: they entered their name themselves |
| Already your customer | Nothing – press <Ui>Continue</Ui> |

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “News by email needs an email address” | Type the email or untick the box. |
| “This card is not registered yet” | A new plastic card was scanned. Type the customer's phone and register it. |
| No name fields | The customer already has a Loyalty.lt account. Only they can change their name. |

<TillVersion />

The till tells you about a new version itself: at start, and then every 15 minutes on a computer and in a browser, every 6 hours on a phone. On the new version card, press <Ui>Update</Ui>.

| Where | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Windows and macOS | The till downloads, installs and reopens itself. |
| iPhone, iPad | The App Store opens. |
| Android | Google Play opens. |
| Browser | The page reloads. |

An unfinished sale is parked before the update and is not lost.

## If it fails

Press <Ui>Retry</Ui> or <Ui>Download</Ui> and install the file yourself. You see your version in <Path>Settings → App Version</Path> and at the bottom of the sign-in screen.

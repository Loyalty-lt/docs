Everyone works under their own name. The first time you sign in with your staff code, after that with your PIN.

<Step>

## Switch staff

At the top, press <Ui>Switch staff</Ui>. An unfinished sale is parked, so the next person does not record it under their name.

## The till locked itself

If your manager turned the lock on, the till asks for the PIN after the set time without activity. The manager sets the time in the portal – see [Shops](/partners/shops#till-quick-keys-and-lock).

## Forgot the PIN

Press <Ui>Use staff code</Ui>, sign in with your staff code and create a new PIN. If you have no code, your manager presses <Ui>Reset PIN</Ui> in the portal.

## Device code

Every till has a code, for example “K7M-3QX”. You see it on the sign-in screen and at the bottom left. With it, your manager sees in the portal who worked at which till.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| “Wrong PIN” | Try again. After 5 mistakes the PIN is locked for 15 minutes. |
| Too many wrong attempts | Wait 15 minutes or sign in with your staff code. |
| “Sign in with your staff code on this device” | There is no PIN of yours on this till yet, or your manager reset it. |
| “TEST ENVIRONMENT – real codes do not work” | Press <Ui>Change</Ui> and choose production. |
| “No Connection” | Check the internet connection. |

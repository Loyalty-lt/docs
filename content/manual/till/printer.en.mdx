A receipt printer is optional. Thermal printers work: USB, network and Bluetooth.

Open <Path>Settings → Printer settings</Path>.

<Shot id="kasa-spausdintuvas" />

<Steps>
<Step>

Turn the printer on and press the button that searches for printers. If it finds none, add the printer by its IP address.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose the paper width and save.

</Step>
<Step>

Print a test receipt.

</Step>
</Steps>

To print a receipt automatically after each sale, turn on automatic receipt printing.

<Callout type="warn" title="This is not a fiscal receipt">
The till prints a loyalty receipt. Give the customer the fiscal receipt from your cash register.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The till cannot connect to the printer | Check that it is on and in the same network. |
| Symbols print instead of Lithuanian letters | Change the printer model in the settings. |

The module works with PrestaShop 1.7.7, 8 and 9. Download it from the [Loyalty.lt website](https://loyalty.lt/en/business#integrations).

## Install

<Steps>
<Step>

In the shop's back office open "Modules → Module Manager".

</Step>
<Step>

Press "Upload a module" and choose the zip file you downloaded.

</Step>
<Step>

When the module is installed, press "Configure".

</Step>
</Steps>

Updating works the same way: upload the new zip file. The settings stay.

## Connect the shop

<Steps>
<Step>

Create an API key in the partner portal. See [API keys](/partners/api-keys).

</Step>
<Step>

In the "API settings" tab enter the API key and the API secret and choose the environment. Press "Save and check".

</Step>
<Step>

In the "General settings" tab choose the shop and the loyalty card. Press "Save and refresh the rules".

</Step>
<Step>

The "Overview" tab must say "Connected".

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="The key must match the environment">
A test key does not work with the live environment, and the other way round. If they are mixed up you see "Not connected".
</Callout>

## Choose what the shopper sees

In "General settings" you can switch every part on and off separately: QR sign-in, points badges, spending points, coupons, the tier discount, stamps and the app invitation.

Discounts appear in the cart as separate lines. If the customer removes such a line, the points or the coupon go back to them.

<Callout type="warn" title="The invitation costs money">
Every invitation is charged as a text message.
</Callout>

## Set when points are awarded

In the same tab choose the order statuses for awarding, cancelling and refunding points. Points are usually awarded when the order is delivered.

The order page has a "Loyalty.lt" panel. It shows what was sent and how many points were awarded.

## Export products to the app

Open the "Product export" tab. Link the shop's categories to Loyalty.lt categories and press "Export now".

For the export to run by itself, the "Overview" tab shows a cron address. Give it to the person who maintains your site.

See [Products](/partners/products).

## If something went wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Not connected" | Check the key, the secret and the environment. |
| The boxes are missing in the shop | Clear the shop's cache. After changing the theme open the module's settings – the module hooks itself in again. |
| "Customer has no Loyalty.lt card" | The customer has no account yet. Points are awarded once they register with the same e-mail. |
| No points are awarded | Check that the order reached the status you chose for awarding points. |
| The product export failed | Your plan must include products in the app. See [Plan and invoices](/partners/billing). |
